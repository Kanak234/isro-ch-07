ISRO Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon 2026 (BAH 2026)
Challenge 07 — AI-enabled Exoplanet Detection from TESS Light Curves

---------------------------------------------------------
PROJECT: exopipe
---------------------------------------------------------
An end-to-end AI pipeline that detects, classifies, and
characterises exoplanet transit signals in noisy TESS
light curves from crowded fields.

Capabilities:
  - Periodic dip detection           (Box Least Squares)
  - 4-class signal classification    (transit/eclipse/blend/other)
      * gradient-boosted trees on physical features
      * AstroNet-style 1-D CNN on phase-folded views
      * probabilistic ensemble of the two
  - Signal-to-noise / significance estimation
  - Transit parameter fitting with uncertainties (MCMC):
      * orbital period
      * transit duration
      * transit depth
      * Rp/Rs, a/Rs, inclination
  - Diagnostic visualization of every analysed light curve
  - Per-detection confidence level

Status: Production-ready for BAH 2026 submission.
